# UAV Weather Operating Limits

Small unmanned aerial vehicles (sUAVs) are typically sensitive to wind and gust conditions.
Operational guidance from civil aviation authorities indicates that wind speeds above 10 m/s
may significantly degrade controllability, especially during takeoff, landing, and low-altitude
urban operations.

Gusts exceeding 15 m/s introduce additional instability and may result in loss of positional
accuracy, increased power consumption, and elevated collision risk near structures.
